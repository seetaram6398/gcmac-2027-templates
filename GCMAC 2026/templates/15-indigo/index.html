01 · Welcome

A global confluence of ideas, innovation and inspiration.

The Institute of Cost Accountants of India extends a warm welcome to delegates, professional colleagues, corporate leaders, academicians, policymakers, standard-setters and distinguished guests from across the world.

It is a matter of great pride for The Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI) to extend a warm welcome to all esteemed delegates, professional colleagues, corporate leaders, academicians, policymakers, standard-setters, and distinguished guests from across the world to the Global Cost and Management Accountants' Convention (GCMAC) 2027, being organized from 12 to 14 February 2027 at The Ashok Hotel, New Delhi, on the theme: “Strategic CMAs for a Resilient World: Leadership, Governance and Sustainable Value”.

The Global Convention stands as the most prestigious international gathering of the Institute, bringing together visionary leaders, global standard-setters and domain experts to deliberate on the evolving role of CMAs in navigating global macroeconomic turbulence, fostering sustainable growth and driving enterprise resilience. At a time when the world economy is confronting unprecedented disruptions, from geopolitical shifts and supply chain volatilities to the rapid adoption of artificial intelligence, the Convention underscores the urgent imperative for our profession to lead with strategic foresight, integrity and decisive governance.

To address these global challenges, the Convention deliberations are anchored on six core thematic layers: Global CMA Frameworks for Value Governance; Ensuring Supply Chain Resilience; Reconfiguring Energy Sources; Sustainable Development Strategies; Global CMA Practices in Service Sector Dynamics; and Harnessing Capabilities of AI-Driven Digital Transformation.

The above six thematic tracks are thoughtfully designed to unlock new opportunities for CMAs in emerging domains, positioning the CMA not merely as a cost analyst, but as a strategic co-pilot to corporate leaders and policymakers alike. The Convention aims to pave the way for strengthened regulatory dialogue, enhanced industry engagement and a re-imagined global paradigm for management accounting.

New Delhi, the vibrant national capital of India and hub of international diplomacy, governance and economic policy, offers the perfect backdrop for this landmark global gathering. This Convention is a confluence of ideas, innovation and inspiration. With distinguished speakers from international accounting bodies, multilateral organizations, ministries, regulators and multinational corporations, GCMAC 2027 aims to define the direction for Strategic Decision Leadership, Value Governance & Public Accountability, Supply Chain & Energy Transition Resilience, Sustainability, ESG & Carbon Assurance and AI-Enabled Autonomous Finance & Digital Transformation.

The Convention will feature thought-provoking technical sessions, plenaries, global fireside chats and high-level networking opportunities that will empower participants with actionable, future-ready insights. Let us come together to use this premier international platform to learn, collaborate, innovate, and lead as catalysts of global economic resilience. We look forward to your valued presence and active participation at GCMAC 2027.

03 · Agenda

Six thematic pillars

Leadership, Governance and Sustainable Value — anchored on six thematic layers designed for a changing global economy.

01

Global CMA Frameworks for Value Governance

Govern what counts · Creating lasting value

Cost and Management Accounting frameworks have been deployed globally ever since the Second World War for effective implementation of public policies by various governing bodies and regulatory authorities. This session examines current global CMA practices in implementing government programmes and policies in selective developed economies — from healthcare delivery in the UK to public procurement in the USA.

02

Ensuring Supply Chain Resilience

Reconfiguring value chains · Predictability and economy

Pandemics, conflicts, volatile currency and commodity markets, and trade realignments have exposed how fragile global supply networks are. Nations are reshoring, diversifying and building buffers, often at considerable cost. This pillar explores how pricing of risk balances efficiency with security through cost intelligence.

03

Reconfiguring Energy Sources

Balancing energy security and sustainability

The current geopolitical disruption in sourcing conventional fossil fuels is forcing a shift to renewables, green hydrogen and nuclear, reshaping tariffs, public finances and industrial competitiveness. Each transition decision depends on a sound cost of capital, subsidy and lifecycle. This pillar addresses the Management Accountant's role in ensuring the energy shift is affordable, bankable and fair.

04

Sustainable Development Strategies

Growth the next generation can afford

Net-zero pledges and the SDGs are moving from intent to scrutiny. Governments and businesses are now asked whether commitments are funded, measured and met. Integrated thinking and cost and management accounting are two sides of the same coin. This pillar focuses on embedding sustainability into costing, budgeting and performance reporting, so that growth and responsibility advance together.

05

Global CMA Practices in Service Sector Dynamics

Transforming measurement in services

Services account for the largest share of global output and employment, from finance and healthcare to logistics and digital platforms. Yet measuring people-driven processes remains one of the profession's hardest challenges. This pillar shares global practices in service costing, pricing and productivity, including their relevance to public service delivery.

06

Harnessing Capabilities of AI-Driven Digital Transformation

Intelligent insights · Strategic decisions

Digital technologies including AI are changing how organisations forecast, decide and control. Process-driven systems are the foundation of Cost and Management Accounting and make it more suitable for leading digital transformation — while leveraging AI capabilities with judgement, ethics and accountability at the centre. This pillar positions CMAs as the link between algorithms and actionable decisions.

09 · About

The Institute of Cost Accountants of India

The Institute of Cost Accountants of India is a statutory body established under an Act of Parliament in 1959. As part of its obligations, the Institute regulates the profession of Cost and Management Accountancy, enrols students for its courses, provides coaching facilities to the students, organises professional development programmes for the members and undertakes research programmes in the field of Cost and Management Accountancy. The Institute pursues the vision of cost competitiveness, cost management, efficient use of resources and a structured approach to cost accounting as the key drivers of the profession.

In today's world, the profession of conventional accounting and auditing has taken a back seat and cost and management accountants are increasingly contributing toward the management of scarce resources and apply strategic decisions. After an amendment passed by Parliament of India, the Institute is now renamed as “The Institute of Cost Accountants of India” from “The Institute of Cost and Works Accountants of India”. The Institute is the largest Cost & Management Accounting body in the world, having approximately 6,00,000 students and 1,00,000 qualified professionals all over the globe. It operates through four regional councils at Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata and Delhi, and 113 Chapters across the country as well as 11 Overseas Centres, functioning under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India.
