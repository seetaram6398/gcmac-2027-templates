Destination

Discover Delhi & Agra

Delhi, a city where thousands of years of history blend seamlessly with the vibrancy of modern life, offers visitors an unforgettable journey through heritage, spirituality, culture, shopping and governance.

Beyond the city, explore Agra's iconic Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri — celebrated for their outstanding Mughal architecture and UNESCO World Heritage status. Within Delhi, the Delhi Metro, DTC buses, auto-rickshaws and app-based transport make the city's diverse attractions easy to experience.
