Getting here

How to reach The Ashok

Chanakyapuri sits in the heart of New Delhi's diplomatic quarter, with easy access by air, metro, rail and road.

By Air

Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) is approx. 14 km away — about 30–45 minutes by taxi.

By Metro

Lok Kalyan Marg Metro Station (Yellow Line) is approx. 2.5 km from the venue.

By Train

New Delhi Railway Station is approx. 6 km; Hazrat Nizamuddin is approx. 9 km.

By Road

Well connected via Niti Marg and Ring Road; taxis, autos and app-based cabs are readily available.

Nearby landmarks: Rashtrapati Bhavan, India Gate, Kartavya Path and the National Museum are a short drive away.
