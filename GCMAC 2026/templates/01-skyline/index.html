Welcome Message

A global confluence of ideas, innovation and inspiration.

It is a matter of great pride for The Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI) to extend a warm welcome to all esteemed delegates, professional colleagues, corporate leaders, academicians, policymakers, standard-setters and distinguished guests from across the world to GCMAC 2027.

The Global Convention stands as the most prestigious international gathering of the Institute, bringing together visionary leaders, global standard-setters and domain experts to deliberate on the evolving role of CMAs in navigating global macroeconomic turbulence, fostering sustainable growth and driving enterprise resilience.

At a time when the world economy is confronting unprecedented disruptions — from geopolitical shifts and supply chain volatilities to the rapid adoption of artificial intelligence — the Convention underscores the urgent imperative for our profession to lead with strategic foresight, integrity and decisive governance.

New Delhi, the vibrant national capital of India and hub of international diplomacy, governance and economic policy, offers the perfect backdrop for this landmark global gathering.

The Convention Agenda

Six thematic pillars

Thoughtfully designed to unlock new opportunities for CMAs in emerging domains — positioning the CMA not merely as a cost analyst, but as a strategic co-pilot to corporate leaders and policymakers alike.

01

Global CMA Frameworks for Value Governance

Govern what counts · Creating lasting value

Cost and Management Accounting frameworks have been deployed globally since the Second World War for effective implementation of public policies. This pillar examines global CMA practices in implementing government programmes and policies across developed economies.

02

Ensuring Supply Chain Resilience

Reconfiguring value chains · Predictability and economy

Pandemics, conflicts, volatile currency and commodity markets, and trade realignments have exposed how fragile global supply networks are. This pillar explores how pricing of risk balances efficiency with security through cost intelligence.

03

Reconfiguring Energy Sources

Balancing energy security and sustainability

The shift from conventional fossil fuels to renewables, green hydrogen and nuclear is reshaping tariffs, public finances and industrial competitiveness. This pillar addresses the Management Accountant's role in making the energy shift affordable, bankable and fair.

04

Sustainable Development Strategies

Growth the next generation can afford

Net-zero pledges and the SDGs are moving from intent to scrutiny. This pillar focuses on embedding sustainability into costing, budgeting and performance reporting, so that growth and responsibility advance together.

05

Global CMA Practices in Service Sector Dynamics

Transforming measurement in services

Services account for the largest share of global output and employment. Yet measuring people-driven processes remains one of the profession's hardest challenges. This pillar shares global practices in service costing, pricing and productivity.

06

Harnessing Capabilities of AI-Driven Digital Transformation

Intelligent insights · Strategic decisions

Digital technologies including AI are changing how organisations forecast, decide and control. This pillar positions CMAs as the link between algorithms and actionable decisions — keeping judgement, ethics and accountability at the centre.

About the Institute

The Institute of Cost Accountants of India

A statutory body established under an Act of Parliament in 1959. The Institute regulates the profession of Cost and Management Accountancy, enrols students, provides coaching facilities, organises professional development programmes and undertakes research in the field of Cost and Management Accountancy.

With approximately 6,00,000 students and 1,00,000 qualified professionals across the world, it is the largest Cost & Management Accounting body in the world. The Institute operates through four regional councils — Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata and Delhi — 113 chapters across India and 11 overseas centres, under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Government of India.
