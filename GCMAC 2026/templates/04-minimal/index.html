01 / Welcome

A global confluence of ideas, innovation and inspiration.

The Institute of Cost Accountants of India welcomes delegates, professional colleagues, corporate leaders, academicians, policymakers and distinguished guests from across the world to GCMAC 2027.

As economies navigate turbulence, technology shifts and sustainability imperatives, the CMA profession is being called to lead with insight, integrity and impact. GCMAC 2027 is designed as a platform for those conversations.

02 / Agenda

Six thematic pillars

Designed to unlock new opportunities for CMAs in emerging domains — positioning the CMA as a strategic co-pilot to leaders and policymakers.

01

Global CMA Frameworks for Value Governance

Govern what counts and create lasting value through globally relevant cost and management accounting frameworks.

02

Ensuring Supply Chain Resilience

Reconfigure value chains and balance efficiency with security through cost intelligence and risk pricing.

03

Reconfiguring Energy Sources

Make the energy transition affordable, bankable and fair — from cost of capital to subsidy and lifecycle.

04

Sustainable Development Strategies

Embed sustainability into costing, budgeting and performance reporting so growth and responsibility advance together.

05

Global CMA Practices in Service Sector Dynamics

Share global practices in service costing, pricing and productivity for people-driven processes.

06

Harnessing Capabilities of AI-Driven Digital Transformation

Position CMAs as the link between algorithms and actionable decisions, with ethics at the centre.
