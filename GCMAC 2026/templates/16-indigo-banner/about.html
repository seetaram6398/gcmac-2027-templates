Welcome Message

A global confluence of ideas, innovation and inspiration.

The Institute of Cost Accountants of India extends a warm welcome to delegates, professional colleagues, corporate leaders, academicians, policymakers, standard-setters and distinguished guests from across the world.

It is a matter of great pride for The Institute of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI) to extend a warm welcome to all esteemed delegates, professional colleagues, corporate leaders, academicians, policymakers, standard-setters, and distinguished guests from across the world to the Global Cost and Management Accountants' Convention (GCMAC) 2027, being organized from 12 to 14 February 2027 at The Ashok Hotel, New Delhi, on the theme: “Strategic CMAs for a Resilient World: Leadership, Governance and Sustainable Value”.

The Global Convention stands as the most prestigious international gathering of the Institute, bringing together visionary leaders, global standard-setters and domain experts to deliberate on the evolving role of CMAs in navigating global macroeconomic turbulence, fostering sustainable growth and driving enterprise resilience. At a time when the world economy is confronting unprecedented disruptions, from geopolitical shifts and supply chain volatilities to the rapid adoption of artificial intelligence, the Convention underscores the urgent imperative for our profession to lead with strategic foresight, integrity and decisive governance.

To address these global challenges, the Convention deliberations are anchored on six core thematic layers: Global CMA Frameworks for Value Governance; Ensuring Supply Chain Resilience; Reconfiguring Energy Sources; Sustainable Development Strategies; Global CMA Practices in Service Sector Dynamics; and Harnessing Capabilities of AI-Driven Digital Transformation.

The above six thematic tracks are thoughtfully designed to unlock new opportunities for CMAs in emerging domains, positioning the CMA not merely as a cost analyst, but as a strategic co-pilot to corporate leaders and policymakers alike. The Convention aims to pave the way for strengthened regulatory dialogue, enhanced industry engagement and a re-imagined global paradigm for management accounting.

New Delhi, the vibrant national capital of India and hub of international diplomacy, governance and economic policy, offers the perfect backdrop for this landmark global gathering. This Convention is a confluence of ideas, innovation and inspiration. With distinguished speakers from international accounting bodies, multilateral organizations, ministries, regulators and multinational corporations, GCMAC 2027 aims to define the direction for Strategic Decision Leadership, Value Governance & Public Accountability, Supply Chain & Energy Transition Resilience, Sustainability, ESG & Carbon Assurance and AI-Enabled Autonomous Finance & Digital Transformation.

The Convention will feature thought-provoking technical sessions, plenaries, global fireside chats and high-level networking opportunities that will empower participants with actionable, future-ready insights. Let us come together to use this premier international platform to learn, collaborate, innovate, and lead as catalysts of global economic resilience. We look forward to your valued presence and active participation at GCMAC 2027.
