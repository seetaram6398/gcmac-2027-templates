Programme & Thematic Pillars

Leadership, Governance and Sustainable Value — anchored on six thematic layers designed for a changing global economy.

Convention Theme

Strategic CMAs for a Resilient World

Leadership, Governance and Sustainable Value — anchored on six thematic layers designed for a changing global economy.

01

Global CMA Frameworks for Value Governance

Govern what counts · Creating lasting value

Cost and Management Accounting frameworks have been deployed globally ever since the Second World War for effective implementation of public policies by various governing bodies and regulatory authorities. This session examines current global CMA practices in implementing government programmes and policies in selective developed economies — from healthcare delivery in the UK to public procurement in the USA.

02

Ensuring Supply Chain Resilience

Reconfiguring value chains · Predictability and economy

Pandemics, conflicts, volatile currency and commodity markets, and trade realignments have exposed how fragile global supply networks are. Nations are reshoring, diversifying and building buffers, often at considerable cost. This pillar explores how pricing of risk balances efficiency with security through cost intelligence.

03

Reconfiguring Energy Sources

Balancing energy security and sustainability

The current geopolitical disruption in sourcing conventional fossil fuels is forcing a shift to renewables, green hydrogen and nuclear, reshaping tariffs, public finances and industrial competitiveness. Each transition decision depends on a sound cost of capital, subsidy and lifecycle. This pillar addresses the Management Accountant's role in ensuring the energy shift is affordable, bankable and fair.

04

Sustainable Development Strategies

Growth the next generation can afford

Net-zero pledges and the SDGs are moving from intent to scrutiny. Governments and businesses are now asked whether commitments are funded, measured and met. Integrated thinking and cost and management accounting are two sides of the same coin. This pillar focuses on embedding sustainability into costing, budgeting and performance reporting, so that growth and responsibility advance together.

05

Global CMA Practices in Service Sector Dynamics

Transforming measurement in services

Services account for the largest share of global output and employment, from finance and healthcare to logistics and digital platforms. Yet measuring people-driven processes remains one of the profession's hardest challenges. This pillar shares global practices in service costing, pricing and productivity, including their relevance to public service delivery.

06

Harnessing Capabilities of AI-Driven Digital Transformation

Intelligent insights · Strategic decisions

Digital technologies including AI are changing how organisations forecast, decide and control. Process-driven systems are the foundation of Cost and Management Accounting and make it more suitable for leading digital transformation — while leveraging AI capabilities with judgement, ethics and accountability at the centre. This pillar positions CMAs as the link between algorithms and actionable decisions.
